# SmartCart Intelligence — 2-Minute Demonstration Script

Use this step-by-step presentation script when showcasing **SmartCart Intelligence** to recruiters, hiring managers, interviewers, or hackathon judges.

---

## Target Duration: 90 – 120 Seconds

### Step 1: Opening & Problem Statement (0:00 – 0:15)
> *"Hi everyone, today I'm demonstrating **SmartCart Intelligence**, an automated e-commerce price monitoring, comparison, and shopping intelligence platform specifically built for the Indian market.*
> *In India, consumers face extreme price fluctuations across Amazon, Flipkart, Croma, and Reliance Digital, coupled with misleading '70% OFF' tags. SmartCart Intelligence solves this with immutable data provenance, explainable deal scoring, and predictive price forecasting."*

---

### Step 2: Login & Overview Screen (0:15 – 0:30)
1. Navigate to `http://localhost:5173`.
2. Click **"Sign In"** in the top navigation.
3. Click the convenient **"Fill Demo Account"** button (`demo@smartcart.ai` / `SmartCart123!`) and click **"Sign In"**.
4. Point out the **Overview Dashboard**:
   > *"The overview gives shoppers an immediate pulse: active catalog items, personal watchlists, active alert triggers, live retailer feed statuses, and the top high-scoring deals across the market."*

---

### Step 3: Discover & Track Products (0:30 – 0:45)
1. Click **"Discover Products"** in the sidebar.
2. Click the **"Electronics"** category filter or search for `"iPhone"`.
3. Highlight the **"iPhone 16 Pro (128GB - Natural Titanium)"** card:
   > *"Notice that every product displays an immutable provenance badge—here marked `SAMPLE` to transparently disclose that it uses certified market data rather than fabricated numbers.*
4. Click **"Track Price"**, enter a target price of `₹1,15,000`, and click **"Save to Watchlist"**.

---

### Step 4: Multi-Retailer Price Comparison (0:45 – 1:00)
1. Click **"Compare Prices"** in the sidebar.
2. Select **"Sony WH-1000XM5 Noise Cancelling Headphones"** from the selector.
3. Review the side-by-side matrix:
   > *"Here we see identical SKU comparison across Amazon India, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, and Tata CLiQ. The system breaks down base price, delivery fees, and stock status, highlighting the lowest total checkout offer with a Lowest Offer badge."*

---

### Step 5: Deal Intelligence & Explainable Guidance (1:00 – 1:15)
1. Click **"Deal Intelligence"** in the sidebar.
2. Select **"Apple MacBook Air M3 (16GB / 512GB)"**.
3. Highlight the 0–100 gauge score and the **"BUY NOW"** badge:
   > *"Rather than a mysterious black-box rating, our Deal Scorer uses an explainable 5-factor model: Historical Average Proximity (35 pts), All-Time Low Proximity (25 pts), Cross-Retailer Spread (20 pts), MRP Discount (10 pts), and Stock Freshness (10 pts). The system provides clear guidance: BUY NOW, WAIT, or COMPARE FIRST."*

---

### Step 6: Price History & ML Trend Forecast (1:15 – 1:30)
1. Click **"Price History"** in the sidebar.
2. Select **"Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra"**.
3. Show the interactive Recharts timeline with the 30-day moving average and all-time low reference lines.
4. Point out the **ML Price Trend Forecast** section:
   > *"Using linear trend regression with residual standard errors, we project future price trajectories for 7, 14, and 30 days bounded by a 95% confidence interval."*

---

### Step 7: Alerts & Mock Email Outbox (1:30 – 1:45)
1. Click **"Alerts & Notifications"** in the sidebar.
2. Click **"Run Manual Price Scan"** to simulate an automated background price evaluation.
3. Click **"View Mock Outbox"**:
   > *"For seamless local development, our mock email delivery adapter renders the complete transactional HTML alert email right in the browser, showing exactly what the user would receive via SMTP."*

---

### Step 8: Purchase Intelligence & Realized Savings (1:45 – 2:00)
1. Click **"Shopping Planner"** to showcase monthly category budget meters and 30-day recurring replenishment cycles for household staples.
2. Click **"Savings Analytics"** to show verified purchase receipts:
   > *"Finally, SmartCart tracks the complete purchase lifecycle. Consumers log verified purchases to calculate genuine realized savings against both retail MRP and historical maximum prices."*

---

### Step 9: Conclusion
> *"SmartCart Intelligence demonstrates full-stack software engineering with React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind v4, Python FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, background scheduling, predictive modeling, and a 19-test automated test suite. Thank you!"*
